\section*{Reader's guide and epistemic badges}

This paper supports three reading paths. For the conceptual atlas, read
Sections~\ref{sec:taxonomy}--\ref{sec:questions}. For the statistical contract
and predictive protocol, read Sections~\ref{sec:method}--\ref{sec:protocol}.
For domain results, begin with the Transformer and diffusion dossier and then
compare the RL, multi-agent, and predictive-continuation dossiers.

Every central claim can be read as three independent coordinates. Fidelity is
\Badge{blue}{P phenomenological}, \Badge{blue}{S trainable synthetic}, or
\Badge{blue}{N natural/pretrained}. Theory status is \Badge{purple}{T exact},
\Badge{purple}{A asymptotic}, \Badge{purple}{G phenomenological}, or
\Badge{purple}{E empirical}. Evidence is \Badge{green}{E0 descriptive} through
\Badge{green}{E6 externally replicated}. These badges are deliberately not
collapsed into one rank: a large numerical study can remain synthetic, and an
exact result can remain far from a realistic endpoint.
