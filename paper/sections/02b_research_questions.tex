\section{Research questions and dossier map}
\label{sec:questions}

The atlas is organized around seven questions rather than around a list of
available simulators. RQ1 asks when a semantically validated order parameter
survives a deformation. RQ2 asks whether a boundary sharpens, crosses, rounds,
or becomes censored under finite-size scaling. RQ3 asks which modules or
interactions causally support an ordered state. RQ4 asks how optimization and
objective geometry alter the route to that state. RQ5 asks whether a numerical
relation predicts an untouched size, control window, or realism edge. RQ6 asks
which conclusions survive a natural-data or learned endpoint. RQ7 asks which
failure mode---semantic loss, scale dependence, path dependence, or model
misspecification---explains unsuccessful transport.

Each domain dossier uses the same four-part report: \emph{observation} states a
registered estimate; \emph{interpretation} states the narrow physical reading
supported by its null and evidence vector; \emph{not established} prevents a
nearby stronger claim; and \emph{next falsifier} names the experiment that could
change the status. The machine-readable question and implementation registries
link each RQ to runnable PhaseCards while leaving known-but-unimplemented and
unknown cells visible.
