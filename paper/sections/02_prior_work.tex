\section{Related work and positioning}
\label{sec:frontier}

We organize prior work by the question it answers, not by a flat list of model
families. This also separates the present problem---statistical mechanics
\emph{of} learning systems---from machine learning used as a tool for classifying
physical phases.

\subsection{Solvable learning and inference}

The statistical mechanics of learning begins from a specified ensemble and a
limit in which quenched disorder and macroscopic order parameters become
tractable \cite{engel2001statistical,mezard2009information}. Replica, cavity,
state-evolution, and related methods identify information-theoretic thresholds,
algorithmic thresholds, and computationally hard regions in planted inference
problems \cite{zdeborova2016statistical}. These works establish the standard we
adopt: a transition is attached to a state object, a disorder law, a scale, and
an observable. Our atlas does not replace those theories. It records which of
their assumptions survive when a trainable implementation removes a solvable
constraint.

\subsection{Deep networks: signal propagation, kernels, and landscapes}

Statistical-mechanics analyses of deep learning cover expressivity, signal
propagation, random matrices, optimization landscapes, jamming, and
nonequilibrium dynamics \cite{bahri2020statistical}. Mean-field signal
propagation separates ordered and chaotic regimes at initialization
\cite{schoenholz2017deep}; neural tangent kernels describe a wide-network lazy
limit \cite{jacot2018neural}; exact deep-linear dynamics show how representations
and timescales emerge during training \cite{saxe2019mathematical}; and the
jamming perspective relates interpolation and loss geometry
\cite{geiger2019jamming}. Random-feature asymptotics clarify double descent and
generalization in another controlled limit \cite{mei2022generalization}.

These contexts are complementary rather than interchangeable. Initialization
signal propagation is not a terminal trained-model ensemble; kernel regression
is not feature learning; interpolation is not semantic recovery; and a
finite-width optimizer trajectory is not automatically an equilibrium sample.
The atlas therefore records theory status and state object separately.

\subsection{Modern architectures and driven systems}

For attention, a solvable tied low-rank model exhibits a
positional--semantic transition \cite{cui2024phase}, and attention-indexed models
extend Bayes-optimal analysis to full-width matrices
\cite{boncoraglio2026aim}. The unresolved transport question is whether order
semantics and a boundary persist under standard feed-forward blocks,
normalization, causal objectives, optimization, and natural carriers. Width,
depth, context, and data are distinct deformations; compute-optimal scaling and
hyperparameter transfer do not make them one thermodynamic coordinate
\cite{tensor_chinchilla_hoffmann2022,tensor_mup_yang2022}.

Beyond supervised learning the same questions recur with different order
parameters: diffusion models separate early speciation from later collapse
toward memorised samples \cite{biroli2024dynamical,ho2020ddpm,peebles2023dit},
reward-model overoptimization requires proxy reward, gold reward, KL pressure
and strategy support to be measured jointly
\cite{gao2023reward,rafailov2024overoptimization}, and multi-agent systems add
field, coupling, topology and population coordinates
\cite{degroot1974consensus,lowe2017maddpg,du2024debate}. None of these is part
of the present release. They are expressible in the coordinates of
\S\ref{sec:taxonomy}, and naming them here is a statement about the grammar's
reach, not about this paper's coverage; recent claims in those areas are treated
as hypotheses rather than anchors \cite{zhang2026concurrence,
denobili2026collective,bertalanic2026ringelmann}.

\subsection{Reproducibility and the gap addressed here}

Model cards, datasheets, and reproducibility checklists make assumptions and
artifacts inspectable \cite{mitchell2019modelcards,gebru2021datasheets,
pineau2021reproducibility}. Preregistration separates exploratory choices from
confirmatory tests \cite{nosek2018preregistration}. None of these alone supplies
a statistical-mechanics ontology: they do not specify which randomness defines
susceptibility, which component is the finite-size coordinate, or when a
threshold is censored. Conversely, a phase diagram alone does not encode
holdouts, provenance, or invalidation. The present work joins these two
traditions through a typed condition record and an evidence vector.

\subsection{Two cited claims that this release tests}

Two of the results above are usually cited rather than re-measured, and both are
tested here directly. The positional--semantic transition in attention
\cite{cui2024phase} is measured in \S\ref{sec:sequence} with a causal ablation
of each route in turn, on data in which \emph{both} cues carry a finite
reliability --- the configuration that makes the switch a property of the model
rather than of the data. And the lazy limit of wide networks
\cite{jacot2018neural} is measured, not assumed, through the joint statement
$\delta_\theta\to0$ and $\mathcal A\to1$ of \eqref{eq:lazy}, so that the
regime is an observable of each run.

The positioning is therefore precise. We contribute neither a new universal
critical exponent nor a comprehensive survey of physics-inspired machine
learning theory. We contribute an operational taxonomy for numerical studies of
modern learning systems, a decision procedure willing to return nothing, and a
map of \AtlasTotalFamilies{} settings measured under both.
