\section{Cross-domain dossiers and coverage}
\label{sec:crossdomain}

Cross-domain coverage tests whether the taxonomy can preserve different state
objects and observables. It does not make the domains coequal in evidential
depth (Figure~\ref{fig:coverage-map}).

\subsection{Diffusion trajectories}

The state object is a reverse-time sample trajectory. Reverse time is the
primary control; locality or score deformation is secondary. Registered
observables include speciation, nonlocality, trajectory commitment, and
proximity to memorized samples. The oracle-score anchor separates speciation
from later collapse \cite{biroli2024dynamical}; learned DDPM and DiT models
would add score and architecture error \cite{ho2020ddpm,peebles2023dit}.

\claim{Observation.} The controlled dossier can represent separate speciation
and collapse coordinates and retain censored boundaries. \claim{Interpretation.}
This validates the trajectory PhaseCard and observable namespaces.
\claim{Not established.} The current artifact does not establish a transition
in a learned image model. \claim{Next falsifier.} Freeze the oracle calibration,
train independent learned scores, and evaluate speciation and nearest-training
sample proximity on a held-out largest resolution.

\subsection{Reinforcement learning}

The state object is a policy/reward trajectory under KL pressure
$\beta_{\mathrm{KL}}$ and verifier noise $\epsilon_{\mathrm{ver}}$. Gold reward,
proxy reward, strategy support, KL, and entropy are recorded jointly because
entropy reduction alone cannot distinguish productive specialization from
reward collapse \cite{gao2023reward,rafailov2024overoptimization}.

\claim{Observation.} The controlled map distinguishes a high-proxy/low-gold
region from a supported-strategy region. \claim{Interpretation.} The taxonomy
expresses Goodhart behavior as joint robustness and commitment observables.
\claim{Not established.} It is not evidence for a phase transition in open
RLVR training. \claim{Next falsifier.} Repeat with independent environments,
gold audits, process and outcome reward, and a prospective verifier-noise
holdout.

\subsection{Multi-agent populations}

Intrinsic evidence $h$, social coupling $J$, topology, and population $A$ are
separate coordinates. Truth-conditioned consensus is the primary order; raw
agreement is insufficient because a global false consensus can have maximal
magnetization. Polarization, truth accuracy, message diversity, and error
amplification are companion observables.

\claim{Observation.} Controlled populations separate field-driven truth
alignment, interaction-driven consensus, fragmentation, and amplified error.
\claim{Interpretation.} The PhaseCard prevents consensus from being equated with
correctness. \claim{Not established.} No shared Ising universality class or
scaling law for deployed language-agent teams is claimed. \claim{Next falsifier.}
Vary population and topology prospectively with private evidence and a
truth-labeled external task.
