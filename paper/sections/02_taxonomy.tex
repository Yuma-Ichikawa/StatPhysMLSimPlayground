\section{An orthogonal atlas of numerical statistical mechanics}
\label{sec:taxonomy}

The design space is a graph rather than a fully observed Cartesian tensor. A
node is a scientific specification; an edge applies one registered deformation.
Seven layers prevent axes of different abstraction from being conflated.

\paragraph{Domain.}
The domains are Transformer representations, diffusion trajectories, RL
feedback dynamics, and multi-agent populations.

\paragraph{State object.}
The primary object is a parameter ensemble, representation, optimizer
trajectory, generated sample, policy occupancy, or interacting population.

\paragraph{Deformation.}
Data/environment, architecture/interaction, objective/feedback,
dynamics/optimizer, and lifecycle changes are explicit operators. Attention
and MLP choices are subcoordinates of architecture, not peers of data or scale.

\paragraph{Disorder.}
Initialization, dataset draw, environment instance, evaluation draw, and inner
replica each have a registered role and seed stream. The outer disorder unit is
the independent unit for uncertainty.

\paragraph{Scale.}
$\mathbf N=(d,L,T,H,n,C,\ldots)$ is a vector. One component is declared the
finite-size coordinate and the remaining components follow a registered path;
the scalar word ``size'' alone is insufficient.

\paragraph{Observable and phenomenon.}
An observable records estimator, units, intensive/extensive character, ensemble,
null, and validity range. A phenomenon is then a hypothesis about recovery,
specialization, locality, multiplicity, symmetry breaking, crossover,
criticality, glassiness, dynamical arrest, or collective alignment---never a
synonym for a visually steep curve.

\subsection{What is measured}

An order parameter must retain meaning under deformation. We group observables
by function rather than by domain (Table~\ref{tab:orders}). In particular,
$K_{\rm eff}^{\rm macro}=\exp H(Z)$ counts effective macrostates,
$N_{\rm eff}^{\rm corr}$ discounts correlated components, and
$r_{\rm eff}^{\rm spec}=(\sum_i\lambda_i)^2/\sum_i\lambda_i^2$ is a
spectral effective rank. They are not interchangeable effective numbers.

\subsection{Outcomes and PhaseCards}

An edge has one of 13 registered outcomes: preserved, renormalized, rounded,
split, merged, new regime, hysteretic, path-dependent,
statistical--computational separation, semantic failure, censored without a
crossing, unresolved, or not comparable.
These outcomes are hypotheses adjudicated by diagnostics, not labels assigned
from visual smoothness.

The minimal experimental record is
\begin{equation}
\mathcal P=(D,S,\delta,\omega,\mathbf N,\mathbf g,\mathbf m,
\mathbf R,H,T,F,\mathbf E,O),
\end{equation}
where $D$ is domain, $S$ the state object, $\delta$ deformation, $\omega$
disorder hierarchy, $\mathbf N$ scale vector and path, $\mathbf g$ controls,
$\mathbf m$ observables, $\mathbf R$ resources, $H$ holdout rule, $T$ theory
status, $F$ fidelity, $\mathbf E$ the evidence vector, and $O$ outcome. Full
PhaseCards are machine-readable; representative cards appear in
Section~\ref{sec:protocol}.
